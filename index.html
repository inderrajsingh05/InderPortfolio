03 / Experience

Built around
real work.

Technical experience in a working observatory environment, with an emphasis on reliability, practical problem-solving and clear documentation.

PRL · MOUNT ABU

Telescope Operator Trainee / Mechanical Operations

Supported telescope operation and maintenance at the Physical Research Laboratory’s Mount Abu Observatory, working with 1.2m and 2.5m telescope systems and auxiliary 43cm and 50cm telescopes.

MAINTENANCE

Observatory infrastructure & equipment

Worked with support infrastructure including DG sets, liquid nitrogen plant, chillers, compressors, UPS systems and aluminization plant. Contributed to preventive maintenance, troubleshooting and maintenance documentation.

PROJECT WORK

Components, upgrades & documentation

Contributed to custom component fabrication, a telescope console control upgrade, and the design and assembly of an enclosure for the M2 Mirror Actuator Drive unit. Also worked with scientific instruments and observation-record documentation.
